iDrivePrivate

The private drive — a marque experience that comes to you, beginning and ending at your own driveway.

The Experience

An hour with a remarkable car, on your roads, on your terms.

iDrivePrivate partners with premier clubs and communities to offer their members something no showroom can: the private drive. A remarkable automobile arrives at your home at the appointed hour. A specialist-driver — equal parts performance driver and marque expert — rides alongside. The roads are yours. The hour is yours. The experience is a privilege of the community that hosts it.
